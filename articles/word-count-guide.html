How to Check Word Count for Assignments and Essays

Students · Published 22 November 2026

Many assignments have a word limit, and going over or under it can cost you marks. This guide explains what counts as a word, how to count characters, and how to check your essay quickly.

Almost every school, college, and university assignment has a word limit. Sometimes it is a strict maximum, like 1,000 words. Other times it is a range, like 800 to 900 words. Either way, your teacher expects you to stay close to the target. Checking word count is simple once you know what the tools are actually counting.

What counts as a word?

Most word counters treat any group of characters separated by a space as one word. That means the sentence "Students study every day." has five words, while "Mohak writes essays." has three. Numbers also count as words, so "2026" is one word.

Some cases are less obvious:

  • Hyphenated words like "well-known" are usually counted as one word.
  • Contractions like "don't" are counted as one word.
  • Punctuation alone, such as a dash surrounded by spaces, is often not counted, but tools differ here.
  • Headings and captions may or may not be included. Check your assignment brief.

Words versus characters

Some assignments ask for a character limit instead of a word limit. Character count includes every letter, number, space, and punctuation mark. There is also a version without spaces. Tweets, Instagram captions, and SEO meta descriptions usually use characters, while essays almost always use words.

Quick example: The phrase "Study every day" has 3 words, 16 characters with spaces, and 13 characters without spaces.

How to check word count step by step

Option 1: Use your word processor

Google Docs and Microsoft Word both show a word count. In Google Docs, open the Tools menu and select Word count. In Word, look at the status bar at the bottom, or open the Review tab and click Word Count. This is the most accurate option when you are writing inside a document.

Option 2: Paste your text into an online counter

If you have written your essay in a notes app or copied it from somewhere else, paste it into our free word counter. It shows words, characters with and without spaces, and lines, as you type.

Option 3: Count a sample manually

For a rough estimate, count the words in one line of your text, then multiply by the number of lines. This is slow, but it helps you understand how word count works, especially for a short exam answer.

Examples: how far off can you be?

Say your assignment needs 500 words, and your first draft has 430 words. You need 70 more words. If you add two short examples of about 35 words each, you reach the target without padding the essay. Now imagine your draft has 640 words. You need to remove 140 words, which usually means cutting a repeated point or a long introduction.

Aim for a result within 5 to 10 percent of the limit, unless your teacher says otherwise. For a 500-word essay, that means between 450 and 550 words.

Common mistakes students make

Copying headings and citations without checking

Your word counter may include the title, references, and captions, while your teacher may not. If the assignment says "excluding references", remove them from your count before you submit.

Adding extra spaces to fill the length

Spaces do not count as words, so adding them does nothing. Extra line breaks and filler words also make the essay worse. Add useful content instead.

Forgetting to recheck after editing

Every change you make affects the count. Check the total again after your final edit, not just after the first draft.

Frequently asked questions

How do you count words in an essay?

Count every group of characters separated by a space. Most word processors and online word counters do this automatically.

Does the word count include spaces?

No. Word count counts words, not spaces. Character count can be shown with or without spaces.

Are hyphenated words counted as one or two words?

Usually one word. For example, "well-known" is counted as one word in most tools.

Conclusion

Word count is a small detail, but it shows your teacher that you followed the instructions. Check it in your document, paste your final text into a counter to confirm, and aim for the target range rather than the exact number.
