How to Calculate GST: Formula, Example and Free Calculator

Calculators · Published 15 November 2026

Whether you are billing a client, checking a shop receipt, or filing your accounts, GST is easy to get wrong when the price already includes tax. This guide shows both directions with clear examples.

GST, or Goods and Services Tax, is added to most goods and services sold in India. Small businesses, freelancers, and anyone who buys things online need to handle it correctly. A mistake in one direction can make a bill look cheaper or dearer than it really is.

There are two situations you will meet. In the first, the price you have is before tax, and you need to add GST. In the second, the price already includes GST, and you need to find the original amount and the tax inside it.

The two basic formulas

GST amount = Price before tax × GST rate ÷ 100

Total = Price before tax + GST amount

When the price already includes GST, use these instead:

Price before tax = Total × 100 ÷ (100 + GST rate)

GST amount = Total − Price before tax

Example 1: Adding GST to a price

A consultant charges ₹2,500 for a service, and GST at 18% applies.

  • GST = 2,500 × 18 ÷ 100 = ₹450
  • Total invoice amount = 2,500 + 450 = ₹2,950

Example 2: Finding GST in a price that includes it

A shop displays ₹5,900 for a product, and the price includes 18% GST. How much of that is tax?

  • Price before tax = 5,900 × 100 ÷ 118 = ₹5,000
  • GST = 5,900 − 5,000 = ₹900

A common wrong method is to take 18% of ₹5,900 and call that the GST. That gives ₹1,062, which is too high, because the 18% applies to the base price, not to the final amount.

CGST and SGST: how the tax is split

Within one state, GST is split equally between the central and state governments. At 18%, that means CGST of 9% and SGST of 9%. In the Example 1 invoice, that is ₹225 CGST and ₹225 SGST. When the buyer and seller are in different states, the full amount is charged as IGST instead, which is 18% in this case.

Common mistakes to avoid

Using the wrong base when reversing a price

If a price includes GST, dividing by 1.18 gives the base price. Taking 18% of the total gives a wrong answer.

Forgetting that rates differ by item

Different goods and services have different GST rates, and the rates are revised from time to time. Always check the current rate for the exact item before you invoice.

Rounding too early

Calculate the full value first and round at the end. Rounding each small step can shift the final total by a rupee or two on large invoices.

Frequently asked questions

What is the formula to add GST?

GST amount = price before tax × GST rate ÷ 100, and the total is the price before tax plus the GST amount.

How do I find GST if the price already includes it?

Divide the total by (100 + GST rate) and multiply by 100 to get the base price. The GST is the total minus that base price.

What is the difference between CGST and SGST?

CGST goes to the central government and SGST goes to the state government. For intra-state sales, the GST rate is split equally between them.

Is a GST calculator accurate?

A calculator is accurate if you enter the correct rate. The rate depends on the item, so confirm it before you invoice.

Conclusion

GST is simple once you know which direction you are calculating. Use the add-on formula when you start from a base price, and the reverse formula when the price already includes tax. Check the rate for your item, and keep the rounding for the final step.
