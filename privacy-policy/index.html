1. Information We Handle

MohakTools provides browser-based utilities, calculators, converters, and other online tools. The information involved depends on which page or feature you use.

Information you provide

Some tools require you to enter values or text to produce a result. If you contact us or submit feedback, you may also provide information such as your message and any contact details you choose to include.

Basic technical information

When you visit a website, the hosting provider or other infrastructure services may process technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, requested page, referring page, and request timestamps. This can occur through standard server logs and security systems.

The exact information collected depends on the website's hosting, configuration, and services in use.

2. How Our Tools Handle Data

Many MohakTools utilities are designed to calculate, convert, or process the values you enter. Where a tool performs its work entirely in your browser, the entered information does not need to be sent to our servers to produce the result.

However, data handling can vary by tool and by any external services or integrations involved. Do not assume that every tool operates entirely offline unless its functionality has been verified.

Avoid entering passwords, financial account details, confidential business information, or other sensitive information into a tool unless you have confirmed that it is appropriate for that purpose.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

MohakTools may use browser storage technologies to remember preferences and support website functionality.

  • Local storage: A browser may save preferences, such as your selected website theme, on your device.
  • Session storage: A page may use temporary browser storage for functionality during a browsing session.
  • Cookies: Cookies may be used by the website, its hosting infrastructure, or third-party services if those features are enabled.

Browser-stored preferences generally remain on your device until they are changed, cleared, or removed by the browser. You can manage or clear site data through your browser settings. Clearing it may reset saved preferences or affect some features.

The technologies actually used depend on the current website configuration and the services enabled.

4. How Information Is Used

Information available to the website or its service providers may be used, where applicable, to:

  • Operate and deliver the website and its tools.
  • Remember browser preferences and maintain functionality.
  • Respond to enquiries and feedback.
  • Diagnose errors and improve reliability and usability.
  • Detect abuse, protect website security, and prevent misuse.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not claim to collect every category of information listed here. Actual processing depends on the features and services operating on the website.

5. Third-Party Services

The website may rely on third-party providers for hosting, infrastructure, security, analytics, advertising, or other functionality if those services are configured.

These providers may process information as needed to deliver their services, subject to their own terms and privacy policies. Third-party websites linked from MohakTools operate independently and have their own privacy practices.

We recommend reviewing the relevant third-party privacy policies before using their services. This policy does not establish that any particular analytics or advertising service is currently enabled.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect information handled through the website. However, no website, internet connection, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for using an up-to-date browser, protecting your device, and avoiding the submission of sensitive information where it is not necessary.

7. Data Retention

Browser preferences remain on your device until they are cleared or overwritten. Technical logs, contact messages, and information handled by service providers may be retained for the time needed for their operational, security, support, or legal purposes, according to the relevant service configuration and policies.

Retention periods may differ by data type and provider. We do not promise a specific deletion period for information managed by third-party services.

8. Children's Privacy

MohakTools is a general-purpose utility website and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children in ways that violate applicable privacy laws.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through a contact or feedback feature, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to personal information, such as requesting access, correction, deletion, or information about how it is processed.

You can clear browser storage and cookies through your browser settings. You can also choose not to submit optional information through contact or feedback features, although this may limit our ability to respond.

To make a privacy-related request, use the Contact page. We may need enough information to understand and verify your request where the law permits or requires it.

10. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's features, service providers, or legal requirements change. When an update is made, the revised policy will be published on this page and the “Last updated” date will be changed.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically to stay informed about applicable privacy practices.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to make a privacy-related request, contact us through the MohakTools Contact page.
