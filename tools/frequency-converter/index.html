About the Frequency Converter

The Frequency Converter changes a frequency between hertz, kilohertz, megahertz, gigahertz, terahertz, revolutions per minute and radians per second. Enter the value and choose the units.

Hertz means cycles per second, and the prefixes step by a thousand each time: one kilohertz is a thousand hertz, one gigahertz a billion. That range covers most of the things people ask about, from the 50 Hz of mains electricity to a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi band or a 3 GHz processor clock.

RPM and radians per second are rotational measures, not cycles. To go from RPM to hertz, divide by 60, since a revolution is one cycle and there are sixty seconds in a minute. Radians per second to hertz is a division by 2π, because one revolution is 2π radians.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a hertz?

One cycle per second. A 50 Hz mains supply completes fifty cycles every second.

How do I convert RPM to hertz?

Divide by 60. A motor turning at 3,000 RPM runs at 50 revolutions, and so 50 hertz, per second.

How do I convert radians per second to hertz?

Divide by 2π, about 6.2832, because one full rotation is 2π radians.

What does GHz mean for a processor?

Gigahertz is a billion cycles per second, used as a rough measure of clock speed.

Are Wi-Fi bands in MHz or GHz?

Both. 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz are common, and older bands such as 900 MHz sit lower.

Can it convert period to frequency?

Not directly, but period is the reciprocal of frequency: 50 Hz has a period of 0.02 seconds.
