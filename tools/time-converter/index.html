Time Conversion Formulas

Time conversion is based on the number of seconds in each unit. To convert between two units, multiply the original value by the source unit's number of seconds and divide by the destination unit's number of seconds.

Minutes to seconds Seconds = Minutes × 60
Seconds to minutes Minutes = Seconds ÷ 60
Hours to minutes Minutes = Hours × 60
Hours to seconds Seconds = Hours × 3,600
Days to hours Hours = Days × 24
Weeks to days Days = Weeks × 7
Milliseconds to seconds Seconds = Milliseconds ÷ 1,000
General conversion formula Result = Value × Source seconds ÷ Destination seconds

Understanding Time Units

Small units: nanoseconds, microseconds, and milliseconds

Very small time units are useful in computing, electronics, scientific measurements, and performance testing. One millisecond equals one thousand microseconds, and one microsecond equals one thousand nanoseconds.

Seconds, minutes, and hours

Seconds, minutes, and hours are commonly used to measure activities, durations, travel time, work sessions, and everyday events. One minute contains 60 seconds, and one hour contains 60 minutes.

Days and weeks

Days and weeks are useful for planning schedules, projects, holidays, and longer activities. One standard day contains 24 hours, and one week contains seven days.

Months and years

Calendar months do not all have the same duration. This converter therefore uses fixed durations of 30 days per month and 365 days per year for consistent mathematical conversions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many seconds are in one minute?

One minute equals exactly 60 seconds.

How many minutes are in one hour?

One hour equals 60 minutes, or 3,600 seconds.

How many hours are in one day?

A standard day contains 24 hours, which equals 1,440 minutes or 86,400 seconds.

How many milliseconds are in one second?

One second contains 1,000 milliseconds. One millisecond is one-thousandth of a second.

How many days are in a week?

One week contains seven days, equivalent to 168 hours.

Are months and years exact in this calculator?

The calculator uses fixed durations of 30 days per month and 365 days per year. These are mathematical approximations rather than calendar-aware calculations.

Can I change the number of decimal places?

Yes. Use the Decimal precision dropdown to control how many decimal places are displayed in the main result and all conversion cards.

Does this converter work on mobile devices?

Yes. The page uses a responsive layout designed for mobile phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop browsers.

Does the converter require an internet connection?

The conversion calculations run in your browser and do not require an external conversion API. The website itself must be loaded first.
