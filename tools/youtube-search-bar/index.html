    About this YouTube search tool

    This tool gives you a faster way to search YouTube. Instead of opening YouTube first and then typing your query, you can enter your topic here and go straight to the results. It is useful when you search for the same kind of content often, such as tutorials, music, or short videos.

    You can search three places. YouTube searches all videos. YouTube Music opens music search results. Shorts adds the word "shorts" to your query, so you see short vertical videos first.

    How to use it

    • Choose where to search from the dropdown.
    • Type your topic and press Enter or click Search.
    • Use a quick topic chip if you are not sure what to search for.
    • Open a recent search again with one click.

    Privacy

    Your recent searches are stored only in your browser's local storage. MohakTools does not send your searches to a server. You can clear them anytime with the Clear button.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is this YouTube search tool free?

    Yes. The tool is free to use. It opens YouTube search results, so you can use all YouTube features there.

    Are my searches saved?

    Your last five searches are saved only in your browser. Nothing is sent to MohakTools servers.

    What is the difference between YouTube, Music and Shorts search?

    YouTube searches all videos. YouTube Music searches songs and music content. The Shorts option adds "shorts" to your query to find short vertical videos.

    Does voice search work on every device?

    Voice search depends on your browser. It works in most Chromium-based browsers, such as Chrome and Edge. If your browser does not support it, the microphone button is hidden.
