Frequently asked questions

Can I edit a task after saving it?

Yes. Select Edit on the task to update its title, notes, date, priority, or list.

How do Today, Upcoming, and Overdue work?

Today shows unfinished tasks due today. Upcoming shows unfinished tasks due later. Overdue shows unfinished tasks with a date before today.

Where are my tasks saved?

They are saved in this browser’s local storage on this device. They are not sent to MohakTools and do not sync automatically.

How do I back up or move my list?

Choose Export backup to download a JSON file. On another browser, select Import backup and choose that file.

Can I recover tasks after clearing browser data?

Only if you exported a backup first. Keep the downloaded backup somewhere safe if the list matters to you.
