About the Image Cropper

The Image Cropper cuts an image to the area you want, right in the browser. Load a photo, set the crop box with x, y, width and height, and download the result.

Cropping changes the framing and the aspect ratio at the same time. Cutting a landscape photo to a square for a profile picture removes the sides; cutting to a portrait format removes the sides even more. Whatever falls outside the box is gone, so keep the original file.

Because the work happens on a canvas in your browser, the image never leaves your machine. That is worth knowing for photos you would not upload to a random website, such as identity documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does cropping reduce quality?

The kept area is re-encoded once, so a JPG loses a little sharpness. Cropping does not add pixels, it only removes them.

Can I crop to an exact aspect ratio?

Yes, set width and height to the ratio you need, such as 1:1 for a square or 16:9 for a video thumbnail.

Is my image uploaded?

No. The crop runs on a canvas locally; nothing is sent to a server.

What formats can I export?

PNG or JPG, depending on whether you need transparency.

Can I undo a crop?

Only by reloading the original. Keep a copy before you start.

Why did my crop come out blurry?

Usually because the crop was small and then enlarged. Crop at the size you need, or start from a larger source image.
