What is an aspect ratio?

An aspect ratio describes the proportional relationship between the width and height of an image, video, screen, or other rectangular area. It is usually written as two numbers separated by a colon, such as 16:9 or 4:3.

How to calculate an aspect ratio

Divide both the width and height by their greatest common divisor (GCD). For example, a 1920 × 1080 image simplifies to 16:9. The calculator performs this reduction automatically.

Popular aspect ratios

  • 16:9: Common for modern video, monitors, and television displays.
  • 1:1: Square images and profile pictures.
  • 4:3: Classic displays and some photography formats.
  • 3:2: Common in digital photography.
  • 9:16: Vertical video formats used for mobile-first content.
  • 4:5: Portrait-oriented social media images.

How to resize without changing proportions

Keep the original aspect ratio locked and enter a new width or height. The matching dimension is calculated using the same proportions, helping prevent unwanted stretching or distortion.
