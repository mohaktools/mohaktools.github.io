What Is a Discount Calculator?

A discount calculator determines how much money is deducted from an original price when a percentage discount is applied. It also calculates the final price payable after the discount.

It is useful when comparing sale prices, checking promotional offers, estimating shopping savings, or calculating reduced prices for products and services.

How to Use the Discount Calculator

  1. Select the currency you want to display.
  2. Enter the original price of the product.
  3. Enter the discount percentage.
  4. Select Calculate Discount.
  5. Review the final price, discount amount, and price breakdown.
  6. Select Reset to clear the inputs and start another calculation.

Discount Calculator Formula

The calculator uses two main formulas to determine the discount and the final sale price.

Discount amount

Discount Amount = Original Price × (Discount Percentage ÷ 100)

Final price

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Example calculation

Suppose a product costs ₹2,000 and has a 25% discount.

  • Original price: ₹2,000
  • Discount percentage: 25%
  • Discount amount: ₹500
  • Final price: ₹1,500

How to Calculate a Discount Manually

To calculate a discount manually, divide the discount percentage by 100 and multiply the result by the original price. Subtract that amount from the original price to find the reduced price.

For example, a 10% discount on ₹800 is ₹80. The final price is ₹720 before any additional taxes, fees, or charges.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Discount Calculator free?

Yes. The calculator is free to use and does not require an account.

Can I calculate a 100% discount?

Yes. A 100% discount makes the calculated final price zero, before any additional charges.

Can I use different currencies?

Yes. Select a supported currency to display the results in that currency. Changing the currency changes the display format only; it does not convert exchange rates or change the entered amount.

Does the final price include tax?

No. This calculator applies the discount to the entered original price. Taxes, shipping, and other charges are not included.

Can I calculate discounts on my phone?

Yes. The page is responsive and works on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop screens.

Are my calculations sent to a server?

The discount calculation runs in your browser. This page's calculator script does not send your entered prices to a server. Separately installed analytics or other website features may have their own data practices.

Results are mathematical estimates based on the values entered. The actual checkout price may differ if taxes, shipping fees, exclusions, or other offer conditions apply.
