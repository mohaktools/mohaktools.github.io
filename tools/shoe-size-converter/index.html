About the Shoe Size Converter

The Shoe Size Converter translates a shoe size between US men's, US women's, UK and EU systems, and shows the approximate foot length in centimetres for each. Enter a size and the system it is in.

The systems are not simply offset versions of one another. UK sizes run half a size below US men's, US women's sizes run about one and a half sizes above US men's, and EU sizes use a different scale entirely, based on the length of the last in centimetres rather than on barleycorns. That is why a single number does not translate cleanly across all four.

Brands make this worse. A running shoe from one maker can fit like a half size larger from another, and the same brand may differ between models. Use the conversion as a starting point and check the manufacturer's own size chart, which is usually given in foot length in centimetres.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do US and UK sizes differ?

They use different zero points and increments. UK sizes run roughly half a size below the equivalent US men's size.

How do US women's sizes compare with men's?

Women's sizes are about one and a half sizes higher than the men's size for the same foot, so a women's 8 is close to a men's 6.5.

How do I measure my foot?

Stand on paper, mark the heel and the longest toe, and measure the distance in centimetres. Measure both feet and use the larger.

What is EU sizing based on?

The length of the shoe last in centimetres, which is why EU sizes look like large numbers and do not line up neatly with UK or US sizes.

Are half sizes available everywhere?

In the US and UK, yes. EU sizing typically uses whole sizes only, so you round to the nearest.

Why does the same size feel different between brands?

Lasts, materials and manufacturing vary. The centimetre measurement is the more reliable guide.
