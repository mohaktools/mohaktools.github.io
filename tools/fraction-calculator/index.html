About the Fraction Calculator

The Fraction Calculator adds, subtracts, multiplies and divides two fractions and returns the answer as a simplified fraction alongside its decimal value. Enter the numerator and denominator of each fraction, pick an operation, and the result is reduced to lowest terms automatically.

For addition and subtraction the calculator puts both fractions over a common denominator, combines the numerators, then divides top and bottom by their greatest common divisor. For multiplication it multiplies straight across, and for division it inverts the second fraction and multiplies. The sign is always carried on the numerator, so you will see -3/4 rather than 3/-4.

The decimal output is rounded to six places, which is enough to compare answers but not to treat as exact. A fraction that works out to a whole number is shown as one, so 4/2 appears as 2. Denominators must be non-zero, and the tool works with whole-number numerators and denominators: convert a mixed number such as 2 1/2 into 5/2 before entering it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add fractions with different denominators?

The calculator finds a common denominator by multiplying the two denominators, combines the numerators, and then simplifies. Adding 1/2 and 1/3 gives 5/6.

Does it simplify the answer?

Yes. Both the numerator and denominator are divided by their greatest common divisor, so 6/8 is reported as 3/4.

Can I enter negative fractions?

Yes. Put the minus sign on the numerator. The answer is always normalised so the sign sits on the top, never the bottom.

Can I enter a mixed number like 2 1/2?

Not directly. Convert it to an improper fraction first: 2 1/2 becomes 5/2. The answer can be converted back to a mixed number by hand.

Why does the decimal differ slightly from the fraction?

The decimal is rounded to six places. The fraction is the exact value, so trust the fraction when the two disagree in the last digits.

Why can't the denominator be zero?

Division by zero is undefined, so a fraction with a zero denominator has no value. The calculator stops and asks you to check the input.
