About the Business Days Calculator

The Business Days Calculator counts the working days between two dates. Give it a start and an end date and it returns business days, total calendar days and how many weekends fall inside the range.

A business day here means Monday to Friday. Saturdays and Sundays are counted as weekends, which suits most Indian offices but not every one: some work Saturdays, and government offices follow a different holiday calendar again.

The holiday option subtracts a built-in list of gazetted national holidays. That list covers fixed-date days such as Republic Day, Independence Day and Gandhi Jayanti. Festival holidays that move each year, including Holi, Diwali, Eid and Dussehra, are not in it, so for a precise count subtract those yourself.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it count both the start and end date?

Yes, both dates are included in the range, so a Friday to Monday range counts four calendar days.

How are weekends treated?

Saturday and Sunday are excluded from the business-day count and reported separately.

Which holidays are excluded?

A built-in list of fixed national holidays. Movable festival dates are not included, so subtract them manually if your office closes.

Can I count a range that crosses years?

Yes. The loop walks day by day, so any range works, though very long ranges take longer to compute.

Does it handle a Saturday-only working week?

No. It assumes a Monday to Friday week. For a six-day week, add the Saturdays back from the calendar-day total.

Why does my count differ from my company's leave system?

Because of holiday calendars and regional weekends. Company systems usually follow their own published list.
