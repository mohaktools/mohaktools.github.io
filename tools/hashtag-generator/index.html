About the Hashtag Generator

The Hashtag Generator turns a description or a list of keywords into ready-to-paste hashtags for social media. Describe what the post is about, choose how many tags you want, and it returns a set drawn from the significant words in your text.

It lowercases everything, splits on anything that is not a letter or a number, drops words that are too short or too common to be useful, removes duplicates, and puts a hash in front of what remains. That is why hashtags come out as one unbroken string: spaces are not allowed inside a tag, so a two-word idea becomes a single compound tag.

Use the output as a starting point, not a finished campaign. Tags that are broad and popular are crowded and rarely surface, while very specific tags reach a smaller but more relevant audience. Mixing two or three broad tags with several narrow ones usually performs better than a long list of generic words.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are the hashtags chosen?

The text is split into words, short and common words are dropped, duplicates are removed, and a hash is added to each remaining word.

How many hashtags can I generate?

Set the count field. The tool returns up to that many, fewer if your text does not contain that many distinct usable words.

Why are there no spaces in my hashtags?

A hashtag ends at the first space, so two-word ideas have to be joined into one compound tag to work.

Can I use these on Instagram, X and YouTube?

Yes, the format is the same everywhere. Platform limits on how many tags are worth using differ, so adjust the count accordingly.

Do numbers work in hashtags?

Yes, digits are kept, so a tag like 2026trends is valid.

Will it remove duplicates?

Yes. Each tag appears once even if the word occurs several times in your text.
