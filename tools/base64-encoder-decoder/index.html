Frequently Asked Questions

Is Base64 encryption?

No. Base64 is an encoding scheme and does not protect confidential data.

Does it support Unicode?

Yes. The tool encodes and decodes UTF-8 text.

What is URL-safe Base64?

It uses hyphens and underscores instead of plus and slash, and commonly omits padding.
