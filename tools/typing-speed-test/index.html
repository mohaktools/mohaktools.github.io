About the Typing Speed Test

This free typing speed test measures how quickly and accurately you can reproduce a passage of text. It tracks your typing speed in words per minute, accuracy percentage, and incorrect characters while the test is running.

What is WPM?

WPM stands for words per minute. In standard typing tests, one word is conventionally counted as five characters, including spaces. WPM is calculated from the number of characters typed and the time spent typing.

How is typing accuracy calculated?

Accuracy is calculated by comparing the characters you typed with the corresponding characters in the passage. Correct characters count toward accuracy, while mismatches count as errors. The displayed accuracy updates as you type.

How to use this test

  • Choose a test duration and passage difficulty.
  • Read the displayed passage and type it into the text box.
  • The countdown starts automatically with your first keystroke.
  • Watch your WPM, accuracy, and error count as you type.
  • When time runs out or you finish the passage, review your results.

Tips for improving typing speed

  • Focus on accuracy before trying to increase speed.
  • Practice regularly with a variety of passages.
  • Use a comfortable posture and keep your hands relaxed.
  • Look at the text you are typing rather than searching for individual keys.

Frequently asked questions

Is this typing test free?

Yes. The typing test runs in your browser and does not require an account.

Can I test different typing speeds?

Yes. Choose from 15-second, 30-second, 60-second, or two-minute tests and switch between three passage difficulty levels.

What is a good typing speed?

Typing speed varies with experience, language, and task. Comparing your own results across repeated tests can help you track progress.
