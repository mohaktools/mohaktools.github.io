About MohakTools Mood Tracker

The Mood Tracker is a free online reflection and productivity tool designed to help you record how you feel throughout your daily routine. Instead of relying on memory, you can create simple daily records of your mood, energy and perceived productivity.

Understand your daily patterns

Regular check-ins can help you reflect on how different days feel. You can compare your mood, energy and productivity averages and look back at previous notes to identify patterns that may be worth reflecting on.

A simple and private journaling experience

Your entries are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. This page does not send your journal entries to MohakTools servers. Your records are not automatically synchronized between devices.

Designed for everyday use

The tool works on desktop, tablet and mobile screens. It provides a straightforward check-in experience without requiring an account or registration.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is an online Mood Tracker?

A Mood Tracker is a tool that allows you to record how you feel over time. This tool lets you select a daily mood, rate your energy and productivity, and add personal notes.

2. Is the MohakTools Mood Tracker free?

Yes. The Mood Tracker is free to use. You do not need to create an account or purchase a subscription.

3. How are my mood records saved?

Your entries are saved in your browser's localStorage. They remain available in the same browser unless its stored website data is cleared or the records are deleted.

4. Can I track my energy and productivity too?

Yes. Along with mood, you can rate your energy and perceived productivity on a scale from 1 to 5.

5. Can I save more than one check-in per day?

The tool keeps one entry per calendar date. Saving another check-in on the same date updates that day's existing record.

6. Can I download my mood history?

Yes. Select Export CSV to download your saved check-ins in a spreadsheet-compatible CSV file.

7. Does this tool work on mobile devices?

Yes. The interface is responsive and works on modern mobile phones, tablets and desktop browsers.

8. Is this a mental-health diagnostic tool?

No. It is a personal reflection journal and productivity tracker. Its ratings are subjective and should not be interpreted as a clinical assessment or diagnosis.

9. Will my entries synchronize between devices?

No. Entries are saved in the browser on the device you use. You can export a CSV copy to keep a separate backup.

10. What happens if I clear my browser data?

Clearing the browser's website storage may permanently remove your saved entries. Export your records before clearing browser data if you want to keep a copy.
