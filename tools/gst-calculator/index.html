GST Calculation Formulas

1. Adding GST to an amount

GST Amount = Original Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100
Final Amount = Original Amount + GST Amount

2. Removing GST from an inclusive amount

Taxable Amount = Inclusive Amount × 100 ÷ (100 + GST Rate)
GST Amount = Inclusive Amount − Taxable Amount

3. CGST, SGST and IGST

For a qualifying intra-state transaction, CGST and SGST are generally split equally.
CGST Rate = Total GST Rate ÷ 2
SGST Rate = Total GST Rate ÷ 2
For a qualifying inter-state transaction, IGST is generally charged at the applicable integrated GST rate.

The calculator uses the selected transaction type to display the corresponding tax components. Confirm the correct tax treatment for your specific transaction.

GST Calculation Example

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a GST calculator?

A GST calculator estimates the tax amount on a price and shows the taxable value and total price. Depending on the selected options, it can also show CGST, SGST or IGST.

What is the difference between GST inclusive and exclusive?

A GST-exclusive amount does not include GST, so tax is added to it. A GST-inclusive amount already contains GST, so the calculator separates the taxable value from the tax included in the total.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST and IGST?

CGST and SGST generally apply together to qualifying intra-state supplies. IGST generally applies to qualifying inter-state supplies. The applicable treatment depends on the nature and place of supply.

Which GST rates can I calculate?

The calculator includes commonly used selectable rates such as 0%, 0.25%, 3%, 5%, 12%, 18% and 28%, plus a custom-rate option. The applicable rate depends on the goods, services and relevant GST rules; the listed rates are not a determination of tax liability.

Is this GST calculator free?

Yes. The calculator runs in your browser and can be used without creating an account.
