Area conversion formulas

Each unit is converted through its exact square-meter equivalent.

Square meters to square feet

Multiply square meters by approximately 10.7639 to get square feet.

ft² = m² × 10.7639104167

Square feet to square meters

Multiply square feet by 0.09290304 to get square meters.

m² = ft² × 0.09290304

Square meters to acres

Divide square meters by 4,046.8564224 to get acres.

acres = m² ÷ 4046.8564224

Hectares to acres

One hectare is exactly 10,000 square meters, or approximately 2.47105 acres.

acres = hectares × 2.4710538147

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert square feet to square meters?

Multiply the square-foot value by 0.09290304. For example, 100 square feet equals 9.290304 square meters.

How many square feet are in one acre?

One international acre equals exactly 43,560 square feet. It is approximately 4,046.8564224 square meters.

What is the difference between an acre and a hectare?

A hectare is 10,000 square meters. An acre is 4,046.8564224 square meters. One hectare is approximately 2.47105 acres.

Can I convert land measurements with this tool?

Yes. The converter supports square meters, square feet, acres, hectares, square yards, square kilometers, square miles and several smaller area units.

Why can the displayed result have fewer decimal places?

The precision setting controls the maximum number of decimal places. Trailing zeros are removed for easier reading. Calculations use the underlying conversion factors rather than the rounded display.

Is this area converter free?

Yes. This tool runs in your browser and can be used without creating an account.
