About the Week Number Calculator

The Week Number Calculator tells you which ISO week a date falls in, along with the day of the year, days remaining and the quarter.

ISO 8601 defines week 1 as the week containing the first Thursday of the year, and weeks start on Monday. That produces a quirk worth knowing: the last days of December can belong to week 1 of the following year, and the first days of January can belong to week 52 or 53 of the previous one.

This matters in business. Payroll, logistics and European calendars often run on ISO weeks, so a delivery scheduled for 'week 1' may be in December rather than January.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ISO week?

A week running Monday to Sunday, numbered so that week 1 is the week containing the first Thursday of the year.

Why is 1 January sometimes in week 52?

Because ISO weeks follow the Thursday rule, so the first days of January can still belong to the previous year's final week.

What is the day of year?

The count of days since 1 January, where 1 January is day 1.

Why do some years have 53 weeks?

A year has 53 ISO weeks when it starts on a Thursday, or is a leap year starting on a Wednesday.

Does it use Monday or Sunday as the first day?

Monday, which is the ISO standard. US calendars often start the week on Sunday and number differently.

What is the quarter?

The year split into four three-month blocks, so January to March is Q1.
