About the Remove Extra Spaces

The text cleaner helps normalize copied text from documents, websites and spreadsheets. Select the cleanup operations you need, then copy or download the cleaned output.

This free tool is designed to work on desktop, tablet and mobile screens. The page uses the MohakTools centralized theme styles and shared site header and footer scripts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I preserve line breaks?

Yes. Keep the line-break option unchanged and only clean spaces within lines.

What does trim each line do?

It removes spaces and tabs from the beginning and end of every line.

Can blank lines be removed?

Yes. Enable Remove blank lines or choose how many consecutive blank lines to keep.

Is text sent to a server?

No. All cleanup runs locally in your browser.
