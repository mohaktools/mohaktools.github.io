    How these alarms work

    Your browser compares the current time with each alarm about once a second, then plays the sound and shows a full-screen alert. Alarms are stored in this browser only, so they survive a page reload but not a different device.

    Get a reliable alert

    • Keep this tab open and visible — background tabs are slowed down by browsers.
    • Turn device volume up, and tap Test sound once so the browser allows audio.
    • Turn on Keep screen awake for alarms you cannot miss.
    • Allow notifications if you want an alert outside this tab.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does the alarm work when the browser is closed?

    No. This is a browser-based alarm, so the page has to stay open. Closing the tab or shutting the device cancels the alert.

    Are my alarms saved?

    Yes — alarms, sound and snooze settings are kept in this browser's local storage. Clearing site data removes them.

    What happens if I miss an alarm?

    Alarms that passed while the page was closed are skipped, and a repeating alarm simply moves to its next scheduled time instead of ringing late.

    Alarm Clock Online

    MohakTools' online alarm clock is a free, no-sign-up way to set an alarm right in your browser. It runs entirely on your device, so nothing about your alarms is uploaded anywhere, and it works on desktop, tablet and mobile browsers alike.

    What you can do with it

    • Multiple alarms at once — set one for a power nap and another for a meeting; each has its own label, repeat rule and on/off switch.
    • Repeating alarms — every day, weekdays only, weekends only, or any custom set of days such as Monday, Wednesday and Friday.
    • Five alarm sounds with a volume control and a one-tap test, so you know it will be loud enough before you rely on it.
    • Snooze for 5, 9, 10 or 15 minutes, plus a one-tap dismiss.
    • Quick timers — a 5, 10, 15 or 30 minute countdown or one hour, set in a single click.
    • Live countdown to the next alarm, and a 12-hour or 24-hour clock that matches your preference.

    How to set an alarm

    Choose a time in the picker, optionally name the alarm, pick how often it should repeat, then press Set alarm. The alarm appears in the list below with a countdown, and it stays there until you switch it off or delete it. When the time arrives, the page shows a full-screen alert with a snooze and a dismiss button, and plays your chosen sound until you respond.

    When a browser alarm is the right tool

    An online alarm clock is handy for short, focused work sessions, cooking, workouts, reminders during a long meeting, or a nap at your desk. For waking up in the morning, a phone's built-in alarm is still the safer choice, because it keeps working when the phone screen locks and the browser is closed.
