About the Power Converter

The Power Converter changes power between watts, kilowatts, megawatts, mechanical horsepower, metric horsepower, BTU per hour and foot-pounds per minute. Enter the value and choose the units.

The watt is the SI unit, one joule per second. Everything else converts into it: mechanical horsepower is about 745.7 watts, while metric horsepower, marked PS and used across Europe and in Indian car specifications, is 735.5 watts. The difference is small but real, about 1.4 per cent, and it is why the same engine can be quoted with two different power figures.

Power is a rate, not an amount of energy. A 1,000 watt appliance running for one hour uses one kilowatt-hour of energy, which is what your meter records and your bill charges for. Air-conditioner capacity is often given in BTU per hour, where one ton of cooling is about 12,000 BTU per hour or 3.5 kilowatts.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between hp and PS?

Mechanical horsepower is 745.7 watts; metric horsepower, PS, is 735.5 watts. Car brochures in India often quote PS.

How many kW is one horsepower?

About 0.746 kW for mechanical horsepower, or 0.7355 kW for metric horsepower.

What is a BTU per hour?

A rate of energy transfer used for cooling. One ton of air-conditioning is about 12,000 BTU per hour, or 3.5 kW.

Is a watt the same as a watt-hour?

No. A watt is a rate of power; a watt-hour is an amount of energy, one watt sustained for an hour.

Why is my electricity bill in kWh?

Because you are billed for energy used, not for the rate at which you used it. A 1,000 W device for one hour is 1 kWh.

Are the conversions exact?

The horsepower definitions are exact by convention. Displayed results are rounded.
