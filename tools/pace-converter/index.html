About the Pace Converter

Runners measure pace as time per distance, such as 5:30 per kilometre, while cars and cycling apps use speed, such as 18 km/h. This tool converts one into the other. Enter the pace in minutes and seconds, pick whether it is per kilometre or per mile, and it returns speed in km/h and mph along with the pace in both distance units.

Pace and speed are inverses. A faster pace means fewer minutes per distance and a higher speed. For example, 5:00 per kilometre is 12 km/h. Everything runs in your browser, so nothing is uploaded.

Common questions about Pace Converter

How do I convert pace to speed?

Speed in km/h equals 60 divided by the pace in minutes per kilometre.

What is a mile in kilometres?

One mile is 1.609344 kilometres, so a pace per mile is about 1.609 times slower than the same pace per kilometre.

Can I enter seconds greater than 59?

No. Seconds must be between 0 and 59, and minutes can be any whole number.
