Speed Conversion Formulas

Speed conversion uses fixed relationships between measurement units. The following formulas explain how to convert between common speed units.

km/h to mph mph = km/h ÷ 1.609344
mph to km/h km/h = mph × 1.609344
km/h to m/s m/s = km/h ÷ 3.6
m/s to km/h km/h = m/s × 3.6
m/s to ft/s ft/s = m/s × 3.280839895
Knots to km/h km/h = knots × 1.852

Understanding Different Speed Units

Kilometres per hour (km/h)

Kilometres per hour measures the number of kilometres travelled in one hour. It is widely used for road speeds in India and many other countries.

Miles per hour (mph)

Miles per hour measures the number of miles travelled in one hour. It is commonly used for road speeds in the United States and the United Kingdom.

Metres per second (m/s)

Metres per second measures the distance travelled in metres during one second. It is widely used in science, physics, engineering, and technical calculations.

Feet per second (ft/s)

Feet per second expresses the distance travelled in feet each second. It is used in certain engineering, sports, and technical applications.

Knots (kn)

A knot represents one nautical mile per hour. Knots are commonly used in marine navigation and aviation. One knot equals exactly 1.852 kilometres per hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert km/h to mph?

Divide the speed in kilometres per hour by 1.609344. For example, 100 km/h is approximately 62.137119 mph.

How do I convert mph to km/h?

Multiply the speed in miles per hour by 1.609344. For example, 60 mph equals 96.56064 km/h.

How many metres per second are in 1 km/h?

One kilometre per hour equals 1 divided by 3.6 metres per second, or approximately 0.277778 m/s.

How many km/h are in one knot?

One knot equals exactly 1.852 km/h because one international nautical mile is defined as 1,852 metres.

What is the difference between speed and velocity?

Speed describes how quickly an object moves. Velocity describes both how quickly an object moves and its direction. This tool converts speed magnitudes and does not calculate direction.
