About the Land Unit Converter

The Land Unit Converter translates land area between square feet, square metres, square yards, acres, hectares, bigha, guntha and kanal. Enter the figure, choose the two units, and it returns the equivalent area.

The international units are fixed: one acre is 43,560 square feet, one hectare is 10,000 square metres or about 2.471 acres, and one square yard is nine square feet. The traditional Indian units are not fixed. A bigha varies from state to state, sometimes within a state, so this tool uses a specific figure for Uttar Pradesh and another for Rajasthan.

That is the important caveat: never convert bigha without knowing which local definition applies. Guntha is more settled at one fortieth of an acre, about 1,089 square feet, and kanal at one eighth of an acre, about 5,445 square feet. For registration and legal documents, use square feet or square metres, which are what the records actually store.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does a bigha differ from place to place?

It is a traditional unit with no national standard. Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Bihar and Gujarat all use different sizes, so check locally before converting.

How big is a guntha?

One fortieth of an acre, about 1,089 square feet or 101 square metres. It is common in Maharashtra and Karnataka.

How big is a kanal?

One eighth of an acre, about 5,445 square feet. It is used in Jammu and Kashmir, Punjab and Haryana.

How many square feet in an acre?

43,560 square feet, which is about 4,047 square metres.

Which unit should I use on a sale deed?

Square feet or square metres. Registration records use these, and traditional units invite disputes.

Is a hectare the same as an acre?

No. One hectare is about 2.471 acres, so a hectare is substantially larger.
