About the Digital Clock

The clock displays your device's current local time and date. It updates continuously and supports both common clock formats.

Helpful tips

  • 12-hour format includes AM or PM where applicable.
  • 24-hour format runs from 00:00 to 23:59.
  • Check your device's time and time-zone settings if the clock looks incorrect.

Frequently Asked Question

Does this clock synchronize with an online time server?

No. It uses the local date and time supplied by your device.

Digital Clock Online

How to use this tool

Open the tool, choose the available settings, and use the on-screen controls to start or update it. Results and time displays update in the page. For time-sensitive tasks, keep the page open and check your device's clock and browser settings.
