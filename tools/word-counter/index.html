About the Word Counter

The Word Counter analyzes text in your browser and updates its statistics as you type. It is useful for essays, articles, captions, notes and writing with length requirements.

This free tool is designed to work on desktop, tablet and mobile screens. The page uses the MohakTools centralized theme styles and shared site header and footer scripts.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are words counted?

Words are counted as groups of letters or numbers, including words containing apostrophes or hyphens.

Are spaces included in the character count?

The tool shows character totals both with spaces and without spaces.

Does it count lines and paragraphs?

Yes. Lines are based on newline breaks, while paragraphs are non-empty blocks separated by blank lines.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Counting happens locally in your browser; the tool does not send your text to a server.
