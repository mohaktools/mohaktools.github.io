Frequently asked questions

What is a unit converter?

A unit converter changes a measurement from one unit to another equivalent unit, such as kilometres to miles or kilograms to pounds.

Which units can I convert?

This tool supports length, weight and mass, temperature, area, volume, speed, time and digital storage conversions.

Are conversions calculated automatically?

Yes. The result updates when you change the input value, conversion category or either unit.

Is the MohakTools Unit Converter free?

Yes. You can use this browser-based converter without installing software or creating an account.

How does digital storage conversion work?

Digital storage units can use decimal multiples, where 1 KB is 1,000 bytes, or binary multiples, where 1 KiB is 1,024 bytes. This converter keeps these standards distinct.
