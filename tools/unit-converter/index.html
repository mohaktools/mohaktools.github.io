Frequently asked questions

What is a unit converter?

A unit converter changes a measurement from one unit to another equivalent unit, such as kilometres to miles or kilograms to pounds.

Which units can I convert?

This tool supports length, weight and mass, temperature, area, volume, speed, time and digital storage conversions.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply the Celsius value by 9/5 and add 32. For example, 100 °C is 212 °F.

What is the difference between MB and MiB?

A megabyte (MB) is 1,000,000 bytes in the decimal system, while a mebibyte (MiB) is 1,048,576 bytes in the binary system. This converter keeps both standards separate.

Yes. It runs entirely in your browser, needs no account and your values are never sent to a server.
