Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Unix timestamp?

It represents elapsed time from 1 January 1970 UTC, usually in seconds or milliseconds.

How are seconds and milliseconds different?

Millisecond timestamps are typically 1,000 times larger than second timestamps.

Why can local and UTC dates differ?

Local output uses your device's timezone, while UTC output uses Coordinated Universal Time.
