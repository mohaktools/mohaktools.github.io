Percentage Guide

A percentage expresses a number as a fraction of 100. The symbol for percentage is %.

1. Find a percentage

(Percentage ÷ 100) × Number

Example: 20% of 500 = 100.

2. Find what percent

(Part ÷ Total) × 100

Example: 25 out of 200 = 12.5%.

3. Find percentage change

((New − Original) ÷ Original) × 100

A positive result means an increase; a negative result means a decrease. Percentage change is undefined when the original value is zero.

How to Calculate Percentages

A percentage tells you how much a value represents out of 100. The MohakTools Percentage Calculator helps you solve common percentage problems without calculating each step manually. Enter your values in the appropriate calculator to see the result immediately.

How do you calculate X% of a number?

Divide the percentage by 100 and multiply it by the number. For example, 15% of 200 is 30 because 0.15 multiplied by 200 equals 30.

How do you calculate percentage increase or decrease?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide the difference by the original value, and multiply by 100. The sign of the result indicates whether the value increased or decreased. The calculation requires a non-zero original value.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a percentage?

A percentage is a way to express a value as a fraction of 100. For example, 25% is equivalent to 25 out of 100 or 0.25.

How do I find 10% of a number?

Divide the number by 10. For example, 10% of 450 is 45.

Can a percentage be greater than 100%?

Yes. A percentage can exceed 100% when the value being measured is greater than the reference value.

Can I calculate a percentage decrease?

Yes. Enter the original value and the new, lower value in the Percentage Increase or Decrease calculator. The result will show the percentage decrease.
