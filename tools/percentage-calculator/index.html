The three formulas

1. Find a percentage of a number:

(Percentage ÷ 100) × Number

2. What percent one number is of another:

(Part ÷ Total) × 100

3. Percentage increase or decrease:

((New − Original) ÷ Original) × 100

Quick answers

What does 10% mean?

10% means 10 out of every 100, which is the same as 0.1. So 10% of 450 is 45.

Can the answer be more than 100%?

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate X percent of a number?

Divide the percentage by 100 and multiply it by the number. For example, 15% of 200 is 30, because 0.15 × 200 = 30. The first box in this calculator does that step for you.

How do I work out what percent one number is of another?

Divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. For example, 25 out of 200 is 12.5%, because (25 ÷ 200) × 100 = 12.5.

What is the formula for percentage increase or decrease?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the original value, then multiply by 100. A positive answer is an increase and a negative answer is a decrease.

Can a percentage be more than 100?

Yes. A percentage is above 100 when the value being measured is bigger than the reference value, such as a price rising from 100 to 150, which is a 50% increase.

How do I calculate a discount percentage?

Put the original price in the Original Value box and the sale price in the New Value box of the Increase or Decrease calculator. The answer shows the discount as a negative percentage.

It is free and needs no account or sign-up. Everything is calculated in your own browser, so the numbers you type are never uploaded.
