About the Meeting Cost Calculator

The Meeting Cost Calculator estimates the paid time a meeting consumes. It multiplies the number of attendees by their average hourly cost and by the meeting length in hours. The result shows the total cost, the cost of each minute and the share each attendee represents.

Seeing the figure in money often changes how long meetings run. A one-hour meeting with six people at 800 per hour costs 4,800 before any preparation or follow-up time is counted. Use an average loaded cost (salary plus benefits, or a blended team rate) rather than take-home pay. Results are calculated in your browser and nothing is saved.

Common questions about Meeting Cost Calculator

How is meeting cost calculated?

Total cost equals attendees multiplied by the hourly cost multiplied by the length in hours.

What hourly rate should I use?

Use the average hourly cost to your organisation for each attendee, which can include benefits and overheads. Any currency works as long as you use the same one throughout.

Does this include preparation time?

No. Enter the total time people spend on the meeting, and add prep or follow-up time to the minutes if you want it included.
