About the Commission Calculator

The Commission Calculator multiplies your sales total by your commission rate to give the commission earned. If you also enter a base salary, it adds the two together to show total pay for the period.

Commission is usually a percentage of the value you sell, so a 5 percent rate on sales of 50,000 earns 2,500. Enter the rate as a plain number (5 for five percent). Leave the base salary blank or at zero if your pay is commission only. All figures are calculated in your browser and nothing is stored.

Common questions about Commission Calculator

How is commission calculated?

Commission equals total sales multiplied by the commission rate divided by 100.

Should I enter the rate as 5 or 0.05?

Enter it as a percentage, so 5 means five percent.

Is total pay the same as take-home pay?

No. Total pay is base salary plus commission before tax and deductions.
