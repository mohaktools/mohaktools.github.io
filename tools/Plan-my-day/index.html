How it works

  1. Type the item name. For example, "Maths" or "GK revision".
  2. Set From and Till. Pick when you start and when you expect to finish. Times show in 12-hour format, like 4:30 PM.
  3. Click Add. Tasks are listed in order of their From time.
  4. Tick the box when it's done. The count at the top updates.
  5. Export. Click Export, choose a format, and the file downloads.

Tips for a better plan

  • Keep item names short. "Maths chapter 4" is easier to act on than a long sentence.
  • Leave gaps between tasks. A 10-minute break between tasks helps you stay on schedule.
  • Start with the hardest task. Your focus is usually best at the beginning of a session.
  • Plan the next day at night. Five minutes of planning saves guesswork in the morning.
  • Export a copy. A PDF or image of your plan is handy to print or keep on your phone.

About Plan My Day

Plan My Day is a simple daily planner that works in your browser. You write each task, give it a from time and a till time, and the table keeps the whole day in one place. It suits study sessions, work blocks, chores and any day where you want to see when each item starts and ends.

Times are shown in 12-hour format, such as 9:00 AM or 4:30 PM, so the plan is easy to read at a glance. The table follows your site's light and dark mode, so the text stays readable in both.

Your plan is saved in your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded to a server, so your tasks stay on your device. You can export the plan as an SVG image, a PDF for printing, a CSV for spreadsheets, a TXT note, or a JSON file for developers.

Frequently asked questions

Is Plan My Day free to use?

Yes. It's free, works in your browser, and doesn't need an account.

Why do I need to set a From and Till time?

The times show when each task starts and when you expect to finish it. This makes the plan easy to follow and easy to compare with what actually happened.

Are the times in 12-hour or 24-hour format?

The table always shows 12-hour times with AM or PM. Your device may show the time picker in its own format, but the saved plan and the table use 12-hour format.

Is my plan saved if I close the page?

Yes, in the same browser. Your tasks are stored locally, so they're still there when you come back. Clearing your browser data, or opening the page on a different browser or device, will not show them.

Can I edit a task after adding it?

Not yet. To change a task, delete it with the trash button and add it again with the new details.

Which export format should I choose?

Use PDF to print, SVG for a picture you can share, CSV for Excel or Google Sheets, TXT for a quick plain-text note, and JSON if you want the data for a program.

Does the plan work on my phone?

Yes. On small screens each task stacks into a card, with its From and Till times shown underneath.
