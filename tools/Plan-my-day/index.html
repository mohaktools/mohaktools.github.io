How it works

  1. Click the "Type a task" box and write a task, such as "Maths".
  2. Pick a time for it.
  3. Click "Add to my day". Tasks are sorted by time automatically.
  4. Tick a task when it's done, or delete it.
  5. Export the plan as SVG, CSV, PDF, TXT or JSON.

FAQ

Is Plan My Day free?

Yes. It runs in your browser and does not need an account.

Is my plan saved?

Your tasks are saved in this browser, so they're still there when you come back. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Can I edit a task after adding it?

Delete it and add it again with the new details.

Which export should I use?

PDF for printing, SVG for an image, CSV for Excel or Google Sheets, TXT for a plain note, and JSON for developers.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The layout adjusts to small screens.
