How it works

  1. Your plan appears in the colors and text size you selected.
  2. Click the "Type a task" box and write a task, such as "Maths".
  3. Pick a time for it.
  4. Click "Add to my day". Tasks are sorted by time automatically.
  5. Tick a task when it's done, or delete it.
  6. Export the plan as SVG, CSV, PDF, TXT or JSON.

Features

  • Timed tasks: Add any task with a start time and keep your day in order.
  • Accent color: Choose a preset or any color to match your style.
  • Text size: Pick small, medium or large text.
  • Dark and light mode: Use light, dark, or follow your device setting.
  • Five export formats: SVG for images, CSV for spreadsheets, PDF for printing, TXT for plain notes, and JSON for developers.
  • Private: Your plan is saved only in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

FAQ

Is Plan My Day free?

Yes. It runs in your browser and does not need an account.

Is my plan saved?

Your tasks and settings are saved in this browser so they're still there when you return. Clearing your browser data will remove them.

Can I edit a task after adding it?

You can tick it off, delete it, or add a new one. To change a task, delete it and add it again with the new details.

Which export should I use?

Use PDF to print, SVG for an image, CSV for Excel or Google Sheets, TXT for a plain note, and JSON if you want to use the data in code.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The layout adjusts to small screens.
