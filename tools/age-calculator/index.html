What Is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator determines the time between your date of birth and a selected calculation date. It expresses the result in completed years, months, and days rather than estimating your age from the number of years alone.

This calculator also provides total completed months, the number of calendar days between the dates, and information about your next birthday.

How to Use the Age Calculator

  1. Enter your date of birth in the first field.
  2. Keep today's date or select another date in the Age As Of Date field.
  3. Select Calculate Age.
  4. Read your age in years, months, and days, along with the additional results.
  5. Select Reset to clear the inputs and start again.

How Is Your Age Calculated?

The calculator compares the calendar dates and determines the number of complete years, remaining complete months, and remaining days. It accounts for different month lengths and leap years.

Total days are calculated from the calendar-day difference between the two dates. The birth date itself is not counted as an additional elapsed day.

Example

If someone was born on 15 January 2000 and the calculation date is 24 October 2026, their age is 26 years, 9 months, and 9 days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I calculate my age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the Age As Of Date field to a date in the past or future. The selected date must not be earlier than your date of birth.

Does the calculator account for leap years?

Yes. Calendar dates and elapsed days are calculated using JavaScript's date handling, including leap years.

Why can the number of days in a month vary?

Calendar months have different lengths. The calculator uses actual calendar dates instead of treating every month as 30 days.

Is my date of birth uploaded anywhere?

This calculator performs its age calculations directly in your browser. Its calculation script does not send your entered dates to a server.

Can I use this calculator on my phone?

Yes. The page is responsive and adapts to mobile phones, tablets, and desktop screens.
