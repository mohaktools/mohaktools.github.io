Frequently Asked Questions

Is this calculator free?

Yes. You can use the Basic Calculator free of charge.

Can I use my keyboard?

Yes. Enter numbers and arithmetic operators directly. Press Enter or equals to calculate, Backspace to delete, and Escape to clear the current calculation.

Can I enter negative numbers?

Yes. Use the plus-minus key to flip the sign of the number you are entering, or type a minus before the digits. Negative results appear in the display the same way.

What is the difference between AC and the backspace key?

AC clears the whole calculation and starts again from zero, including the pending operator and the previous number. Backspace removes only the last digit you typed, which is what you want after a single typo.

Can I work with percentages?

Yes. The percentage key converts the number in the display to a percentage, which is useful for discounts, tips and tax. For a percentage of a specific amount, multiply first and then apply the percentage key.
