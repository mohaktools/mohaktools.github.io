About the AI Question Generator

This tool creates questions from the ideas and details in your paragraph. Choose a mix of question styles, multiple choice, or short answer, then reveal answers and explanations when you’re ready. Generated questions are a study aid; check them against the original passage for accuracy.

Your paragraph is sent to the configured MohakTools Cloudflare Worker for processing with Groq’s openai/gpt-oss-120b model. The Groq API key stays on the Worker and is never included in this page.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of text can I use?

Paste a paragraph or upload a .txt file up to 5,000 characters. The tool uses only the information in that passage to create questions.

Can I answer multiple-choice questions on the page?

Yes. Select an option for instant feedback, then track your progress as you work through the set. Short-answer questions include a revealable answer.

Can I save the questions as a text file?

Yes. Enter a file name and choose Download as .txt, or copy the full question set to your clipboard.

Are the questions guaranteed to be correct?

No. AI can misunderstand a passage. Review the questions and answers against the source text before using them.
