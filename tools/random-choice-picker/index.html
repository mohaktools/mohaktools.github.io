How to Use the Random Choice Picker

  • Enter your options in the list, with one choice per line.
  • Choose how many options you want to draw.
  • Configure duplicate handling and whether picked choices should be removed.
  • Click Pick Random Choice to run the draw.
  • Copy or export the results and review your pick history.

What Is a Random Choice Picker?

A random choice picker selects one or more entries from a list using a random selection process. It can help you make everyday decisions, choose a team activity, select a name, or organize a simple drawing.

Each entry is treated as a separate option. If duplicate removal is disabled, repeated entries can increase that option's chance of being selected. Enable the relevant settings to change how repeated choices are handled.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Random Choice Picker free?

Yes. You can use the picker for free in your browser.

Can I pick multiple winners?

Yes. Set the number of choices to pick. Enable Avoid repeats within a draw to ensure that the same entry cannot be selected twice in one draw.

Can I remove a selected choice automatically?

Yes. Enable Remove picked choices from the list to remove the selected entries after a successful draw.

How are the results randomized?

The picker uses the browser's cryptographically secure random number generator when available, with a standard random-number fallback in unsupported environments. It is intended for everyday choices, not certified prize drawings or regulated lotteries.

Are my choices saved permanently?

No. The list and history are kept in the current page session. Refreshing or closing the page can reset them. Export your results if you need a separate copy.
