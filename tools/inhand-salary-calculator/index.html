About the In-hand Salary Calculator

The In-hand Salary Calculator turns an annual CTC into the amount that actually reaches your bank account each month. CTC is not salary: it bundles the employer's own contributions to provident fund and gratuity, neither of which you receive monthly, plus your own deductions and income tax.

The calculator first strips the employer's PF contribution, capped at twelve per cent of basic or eighteen hundred rupees a month, whichever is lower, and gratuity, taken at 4.81 per cent of basic. What remains is your gross salary. From the monthly gross it then deducts your own PF contribution on the same basis, professional tax, and income tax computed under the new regime with the seventy-five thousand standard deduction.

The result is a close estimate, not a payslip. Professional tax depends on your state and is at most two thousand five hundred rupees a year, so two hundred a month is a common figure but not a universal one. Employers may also deduct meal cards, insurance premiums or voluntary PF, and some restrict PF to the fifteen-thousand-rupee wage ceiling while others contribute on full basic.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my in-hand less than CTC divided by twelve?

Because CTC includes the employer's PF contribution and gratuity, which you do not get monthly, and your salary is then reduced by your own PF, professional tax and income tax.

How is PF calculated here?

Twelve per cent of basic, capped at ₹1,800 a month, which corresponds to the ₹15,000 statutory wage ceiling. Both the employer and employee shares use the same basis.

Why is professional tax shown as ₹200?

It varies by state and tops out at ₹2,500 a year, so ₹200 a month is a reasonable default. Check your payslip for the actual figure.

Does this include income tax?

Yes, under the new regime with the ₹75,000 standard deduction and the section 87A rebate. If you have opted for the old regime, your tax will usually be lower and in-hand higher.

Is gratuity really part of CTC?

At most employers, yes, typically at 4.81 per cent of basic. It is paid only when you leave after five years, so it is part of the package but not of your monthly salary.

How accurate is the result?

It should land within a few thousand rupees a year for a standard structure. Variable pay, joining bonuses, meal cards and state-specific professional tax all move the final number.
