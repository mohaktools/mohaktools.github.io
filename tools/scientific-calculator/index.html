Scientific Calculator Guide

Use the scientific keypad to enter expressions and evaluate mathematical functions. You can also type supported expressions directly using your keyboard.

Available functions

  • Trigonometry: sin, cos, tan
  • Inverse trigonometry: asin, acos, atan
  • Logarithms: log (base 10) and ln (natural log)
  • Powers and roots: x², xʸ, square root
  • Other functions: absolute value, factorial, exponential
  • Constants: π, e, and Ans (previous answer)
  • Operators: addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and modulo

How to use it

  • Enter a number or select a mathematical function.
  • Use parentheses to group parts of an expression.
  • Choose DEG for degrees or RAD for radians.
  • Press = or Enter to calculate the result.
  • Use AC to clear the expression or DEL to remove the last entry.

Example calculations

  • sqrt(144) = 12
  • log(1000) = 3
  • 5^3 = 125
  • sin(30) = 0.5 in DEG mode
  • 5! = 120

Free Online Scientific Calculator

The MohakTools scientific calculator is designed for students, teachers, and anyone who needs to solve mathematical expressions online. It supports common arithmetic as well as advanced functions such as trigonometry, logarithms, factorials, powers, and roots. No software installation is required.

Degrees and radians

Trigonometric calculations depend on the angle unit. Select DEG when working with degrees, such as sin(30°), or RAD when working with radians, such as sin(π/6). The selected mode also applies to inverse trigonometric functions.

Logarithms, powers, and factorials

Use log for base-10 logarithms and ln for natural logarithms. The power key calculates exponents, while the square-root and factorial keys handle other common scientific operations. Invalid operations, such as division by zero or the logarithm of a non-positive number, are reported rather than displayed as valid numerical answers.
