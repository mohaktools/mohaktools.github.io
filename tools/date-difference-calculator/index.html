How to Use the Date Difference Calculator

  1. Select the start date.
  2. Select the end date.
  3. Choose whether to include both dates in the total day count.
  4. Click Calculate Difference.
  5. Review the calendar difference, total days and total weeks.

The calculator accepts dates in either chronological order. If the end date is earlier than the start date, the duration is calculated using the earlier and later dates, and the direction is identified in the results.

How Is the Date Difference Calculated?

The calendar difference is calculated using calendar years, months and days, rather than assuming that every month has the same length. The total day difference is calculated from the two calendar dates.

Total Days = Later Date − Earlier Date
Total Weeks = Whole Number of Total Days ÷ 7
Remaining Days = Total Days modulo 7

When the inclusive counting option is selected, one day is added to the total day count so that both the start date and end date are included. The calendar duration remains the elapsed calendar difference between the dates.

Example of Date Difference

Suppose the start date is 1 January 2025 and the end date is 1 January 2026.

Calendar Difference = 1 year
Elapsed Calendar Days = 365 days
Total Weeks = 52 weeks and 1 day
Inclusive Day Count = 366 days

The inclusive count is one day greater because it counts both 1 January 2025 and 1 January 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I calculate the difference between dates in different years?

Yes. Enter any two valid dates supported by your browser's date input, and the calculator will calculate the duration across months, years and leap years.

Does the calculator account for leap years?

Yes. The total day calculation uses calendar dates, so leap days are accounted for when they fall within the selected date range.

What does inclusive counting mean?

Inclusive counting adds one day to the elapsed day difference so both the start date and end date are counted. Leave the option unchecked to calculate the ordinary elapsed calendar days.

Does the calculator count business days?

No. This calculator counts calendar days, including weekends and public holidays. Business-day calculations require separate rules for weekends and applicable holidays.

Is the Date Difference Calculator free?

Yes. It runs in your browser and does not require an account.
