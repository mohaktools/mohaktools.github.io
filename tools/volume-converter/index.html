About the Volume Converter

The Volume Converter translates between millilitres, litres, cubic centimetres, cubic metres, US fluid ounces, cups, pints, quarts, gallons, imperial pints and gallons, and tablespoons. Enter the value and choose the units.

The metric side is simple: one cubic centimetre is one millilitre, a litre is a thousand millilitres, and a cubic metre is a thousand litres. The difficulty is that the US and imperial systems share names but not sizes. A US gallon is 3.785 litres while an imperial gallon is 4.546, and the fluid ounces differ too, so a recipe from the UK will not match a US measuring cup.

For cooking, remember that volume and weight are not interchangeable. For engineering and shipping, cubic metres are the standard, and one cubic metre of water weighs a tonne, which is a handy check on tank and container capacities.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a cc the same as a millilitre?

Yes, exactly. One cubic centimetre is one millilitre, which is why engine sizes are quoted in cc and medicines in ml.

How many litres in a US gallon?

3.785 litres. An imperial gallon is larger at 4.546 litres.

How many litres in a cubic metre?

One thousand. That is why water tanks are rated in litres and deliveries in cubic metres.

Why do US and imperial fluid ounces differ?

They are fractions of differently sized gallons, so the US fluid ounce is 29.57 ml and the imperial one is 28.41 ml.

Can I convert grams to millilitres?

Only for water and similar-density liquids. For flour, sugar or oil the density differs and you need the specific figure.

Which gallon does my car use?

In India, neither; fuel is sold in litres. US mpg and UK mpg differ because of the gallon definitions.
