About this tool

This checker applies the Gregorian calendar rule and reports whether February has 28 or 29 days. It also finds the previous and next leap year, with no sign-up or data upload.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if a year is a leap year?

A year divisible by 4 is usually a leap year. Century years are an exception unless they are divisible by 400. That makes 2000 a leap year and 1900 a common year.

How many days are in a leap year?

A leap year has 366 days. February has 29 days instead of 28.

Why do century years have a special rule?

The extra day keeps the calendar aligned with the time Earth takes to orbit the Sun. Skipping most century leap days makes the calendar more accurate over long periods.
