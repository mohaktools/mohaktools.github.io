About this habit tracker

Set daily, weekday, or custom schedules, then use the weekly grid to record completed days. Streaks count consecutive scheduled days completed; a day you did not plan to practice does not break the streak. Data is saved in this browser on this device. Use Export backup to keep a portable copy.

Frequently asked questions

How are streaks counted?

A streak counts consecutive scheduled days that you completed. Days outside the habit’s schedule are skipped. A scheduled day you have not completed can pause the current count until the day is over.

Can I mark a past day?

Yes. Use the week arrows to open an earlier week and tap any scheduled day. Future dates cannot be marked.

Can I change a habit after adding it?

Yes. Use Edit on its row to rename it or change its schedule. Existing check-ins stay attached to the habit.

Where are my habits saved?

In this browser’s local storage on this device. Nothing is uploaded or synced automatically.

How do I move habits to another device?

Export a JSON backup here, then use Import backup on the other device to restore it.
