Frequently asked questions

How do I convert minutes to decimal hours?

Divide the minutes by 60 and add the result to the whole hours. For example, 7 hours 30 minutes is 7 + 30 ÷ 60 = 7.5 hours.

What is 45 minutes as a decimal?

45 minutes is 0.75 decimal hours because 45 ÷ 60 = 0.75.

How are decimal hours converted back to minutes?

The converter multiplies decimal hours by 60, then shows the total as whole hours and remaining minutes.
