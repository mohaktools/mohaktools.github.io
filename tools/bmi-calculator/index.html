What Is Body Mass Index (BMI)?

Body Mass Index, or BMI, is a screening measure calculated from a person's weight and height. It is commonly used alongside other information to help assess weight-related health risks in adults.

BMI does not directly measure body fat, distinguish fat from muscle, or provide a complete picture of a person's health. It should be interpreted in context rather than used as a diagnosis or a measure of personal worth.

How Is BMI Calculated?

The BMI formula uses weight divided by height squared. The calculator converts imperial measurements to metric units before applying the formula.

Metric formula

BMI = Weight in kilograms ÷ (Height in metres × Height in metres).

Imperial formula

BMI = 703 × Weight in pounds ÷ (Height in inches × Height in inches).

The result is a numerical index. It is not a direct measurement of body fat or an individualized health assessment.

Adult BMI Categories

For adults aged 20 and older, commonly used CDC classifications define the following BMI ranges:

  • Below 18.5: Underweight category.
  • 18.5 to below 25: Healthy weight category.
  • 25 to below 30: Overweight category.
  • 30 and above: Obesity category.

These are screening categories, not a diagnosis or a complete assessment of an individual's health. They may not apply equally to every population or individual.

Important Limitations of BMI

BMI is a convenient screening tool, but it has important limitations:

  • It does not distinguish muscle mass from fat mass.
  • It does not show where body fat is distributed.
  • It does not account for every factor affecting health.
  • A BMI value alone cannot establish whether a person is healthy or unhealthy.

A qualified healthcare professional can interpret BMI alongside medical history, physical development, relevant measurements, and other health information.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this BMI calculator free?

Yes. You can use the calculator without paying or creating an account.

Can I use kilograms and centimetres?

Yes. Select Metric to enter weight in kilograms and height in centimetres. Select Imperial to enter pounds, feet, and inches.

Does BMI apply to children and teenagers?

Adult BMI categories should not be used for children or teenagers. For ages 2–19, BMI is interpreted using age- and sex-specific growth charts. A healthcare professional can help interpret those results appropriately.

Does BMI provide a complete picture of health?

No. BMI is a screening measure and does not directly measure body fat or account for every health factor. It should be considered alongside other relevant information.

Are my measurements uploaded to a server?

The calculator performs the BMI calculation locally in your browser. Its calculation code does not send your entered measurements to a server. Other website features, such as analytics, if installed separately, may have their own data handling practices.
