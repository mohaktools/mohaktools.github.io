About the AI Email Writer

This tool turns a short description into a useful first draft. Choose a tone and length, then review and edit the subject and message before copying or sending. AI generated text can make mistakes, so check names, dates, facts, and any commitments in the draft.

Email generation is sent to the configured MohakTools Cloudflare Worker, which calls Groq’s openai/gpt-oss-120b model. Keep your Groq API key in the Worker environment, never in this page. Gmail is optional: the page requests the limited Gmail send permission when you choose to connect, and sends only after you press “Send with Gmail.”

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the generated email sent automatically?

No. The draft stays editable here. It is sent only after you connect Gmail and choose “Send with Gmail.”

Can I edit the AI draft?

Yes. Both the subject and message are editable, and you can copy the draft or send your edited version.

What does connecting Gmail allow?

The page asks Google for the Gmail send permission needed to send a message on your behalf. It does not request access to read your inbox.

What should I check before sending?

Review the recipient, names, facts, links, dates, and tone. AI can misunderstand context or invent details.
