Temperature Conversion Formulas

Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin measure temperature using different scales. These standard formulas convert values between the three systems.

Celsius to Fahrenheit °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
Fahrenheit to Celsius °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9
Celsius to Kelvin K = °C + 273.15
Kelvin to Celsius °C = K − 273.15
Fahrenheit to Kelvin K = (°F − 32) × 5/9 + 273.15
Kelvin to Fahrenheit °F = (K − 273.15) × 9/5 + 32

Understanding the Three Temperature Scales

Celsius (°C)

Celsius is commonly used in everyday life and weather reports in countries such as India. At standard atmospheric pressure, water freezes at 0°C and boils at 100°C.

Fahrenheit (°F)

Fahrenheit is commonly used for everyday temperatures in the United States. Water freezes at 32°F and boils at 212°F at standard atmospheric pressure.

Kelvin (K)

Kelvin is the SI base unit of thermodynamic temperature and is widely used in science. Its scale begins at absolute zero, 0 K. Kelvin values do not use a degree symbol.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply the Celsius temperature by 9/5, then add 32. For example, 25°C equals 77°F.

How do I convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?

Subtract 32 from the Fahrenheit value and multiply the result by 5/9. For example, 68°F equals 20°C.

How do I convert Celsius to Kelvin?

Add 273.15 to the Celsius temperature. For example, 25°C equals 298.15 K.

Can Kelvin temperatures be negative?

No. Kelvin temperatures cannot be below 0 K. The converter rejects values below absolute zero in all three temperature scales.
