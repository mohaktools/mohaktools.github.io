Loan EMI Formula

For a standard loan with a fixed interest rate and equal monthly instalments, EMI is calculated using the following formula:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ [(1 + r)n − 1]

Where:
P = Principal loan amount
r = Monthly interest rate, calculated as annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100
n = Total number of monthly instalments

When the interest rate is 0%, the EMI is the principal divided by the number of instalments. The calculator handles this case separately.

What Affects Your Monthly EMI?

Loan Amount

A larger principal generally results in a higher EMI when the rate and tenure remain unchanged.

Interest Rate

A higher interest rate generally increases the EMI and total interest for the same principal and tenure.

Loan Tenure

A longer tenure usually reduces the monthly instalment but can increase the total interest paid over the life of the loan.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does EMI stand for?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the regular monthly payment made toward a loan, usually covering both principal and interest.

Does the EMI calculator include processing fees?

No. This calculator estimates principal and interest repayments. Processing fees, insurance, taxes, late charges and other lender costs are not included.

Can I use this for a home, car or personal loan?

Yes. You can use it to estimate repayments for different loan types by entering the relevant principal, annual interest rate and tenure. Actual terms vary by lender and product.

Does a longer tenure always mean a lower total cost?

No. A longer tenure may reduce the monthly EMI but often increases the total interest paid. Compare both the monthly payment and total repayment before choosing a loan.

Is this Loan EMI Calculator free?

Yes. The calculator works in your browser and does not require an account.
