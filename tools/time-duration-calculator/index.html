What Is a Time Duration Calculator?

A time duration calculator finds the elapsed time between two clock times. It converts the interval into hours, minutes, and seconds, helping you understand how long an activity lasted without manually subtracting the times.

This calculator is useful for tracking work shifts, measuring study time, calculating event lengths, planning activities, and checking time intervals that cross midnight.

How to Calculate Time Duration

  1. Enter the time when the activity starts.
  2. Enter the time when the activity ends.
  3. If the end time is on the following day, enable the overnight option.
  4. Select whether seconds should appear in the breakdown.
  5. Click Calculate Duration to view the result.

Time Duration Formula

The calculator converts each time into seconds after midnight and subtracts the starting value from the ending value. For an overnight interval, one full day is added to the ending time.

Duration = End time − Start time
Overnight duration = End time + 24 hours − Start time

The total number of seconds is then converted into hours, minutes, and remaining seconds. Decimal hours are calculated by dividing the total seconds by 3,600.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate hours and minutes between two times?

Enter the start and end times, then select Calculate Duration. The result shows the elapsed time in hours, minutes, seconds, total minutes, total seconds, and decimal hours.

Can I calculate time that crosses midnight?

Yes. Enable the overnight calculation option when the end time falls on the next day. For example, 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM is 8 hours.

How do I convert a duration into decimal hours?

Divide the total number of minutes by 60, or divide the total number of seconds by 3,600. For example, 2 hours 30 minutes equals 2.5 hours.

Does the calculator include break time?

No. The result is the elapsed time between the entered times. To find net working time, subtract unpaid breaks from the calculated duration separately.

Can I calculate a duration of zero?

Yes. If the start and end times are identical and overnight mode is disabled, the elapsed duration is zero.

Is this time duration calculator free?

Yes. The calculator runs in your browser and does not require an account or payment.
