About this tool

This calculator offers a quick human-age comparison using a simple rule of thumb. A pet’s actual aging depends on factors such as species, breed, body size, and health, so treat the result as an estimate rather than a medical measure.

Frequently asked questions

Is one pet year always equal to seven human years?

No. Pets mature more quickly early in life, and aging rates differ between cats and dogs and among individual animals. The seven-year rule is a rough simplification.

How does this calculator estimate a dog’s age?

It assigns 15 human years to the first year, 9 to the second, and about 5 for each later year. This simplified approach does not account for breed or size.

How does this calculator estimate a cat’s age?

It assigns 15 human years to the first year, 9 to the second, and about 4 for each later year. The result is only a general comparison.

Can I use this result to assess my pet’s health?

No. This estimate is for general interest. Ask a veterinarian for advice about your pet’s age, health, or care.
