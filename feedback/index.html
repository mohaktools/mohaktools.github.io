What can you share?

Feedback can be short or detailed. Here are a few examples of useful things to tell us:

  • ✓ A new tool or feature you would find useful.
  • ✓ An incorrect result or unexpected tool behaviour.
  • ✓ A confusing label, layout, or usability issue.
  • ✓ An idea that could make an existing tool easier to use.

Reporting a technical issue?

Include the tool name, the steps you took, and the result you expected. Avoid including passwords or sensitive personal information.
